=== SESSION SHARE (src/components/vault/ShareModal.tsx) ===

Modal triggered by "Share" on a vault session card.
  
  Three options presented as tabs:
  
  TAB 1 — "Share with Editor":
    A link to view/download all tracks.
    Text: "Anyone with this link can download your session tracks."
    [Copy Link] button, link displayed in monospace input (read-only)
    Small print: "The link contains everything an editor needs to work on your session."
  
  TAB 2 — "Share Individual Tracks":
    List of per-participant track links, each with their own [Copy] button.
    Useful for sharing just one guest's track back to them.
  
  TAB 3 — "Archive Info":
    Shows the "archive ID" (the naddr, but labeled as "Archive ID")
    "This ID permanently identifies your session. It can be used to verify the
     authenticity of your recordings." (power users will know what this means)

=== ERROR STATES ===

Every error must be:
  - Human-readable ("Couldn't connect to the room")
  - Actionable ("Check your connection and try again [Retry]")
  - Non-alarming (never show stack traces or error codes to users)

Key error screens:
  Expired invite link: "This invite link has expired. Ask the host for a new one."
  Session not found: "We couldn't find this session. It may have ended."
  No camera/mic: "We need access to your camera and microphone to continue."
    + [Open Settings] button linking to chrome://settings/content/camera
  Upload failed: "Some tracks didn't finish syncing. [Download Locally Instead]"
    — always offer a local download fallback
